## Design Principles Applied

1. **Consistency**: Yellow and brown colors used consistently across all interactive elements
2. **Hierarchy**: Primary actions use bright yellow, secondary elements use brown
3. **Contrast**: Brown text on yellow backgrounds and yellow text on brown backgrounds for optimal readability
4. **Accessibility**: Maintained proper color contrast ratios for WCAG compliance
5. **Brand Identity**: Altmann Grotesk font family for a clean, modern feel
